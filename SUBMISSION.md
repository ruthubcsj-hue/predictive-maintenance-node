Predictive Maintenance Node on an STM32F407G-DISC1. It samples the onboard LIS3DSH accelerometer at 100 Hz and the MCU internal temperature sensor through the ADC. It computes a one-second, gravity-removed vibration RMS, learns a baseline over the first ten seconds, and signals status with LEDs: blue = calibrating, green = normal, orange = warning (above 3x baseline), red = anomaly (above 10x baseline, with an optional buzzer output), orange plus blue = sensor fault. The user button restarts calibration. Status is also written as CSV over USART2.

The PRD, requirements, design spec and task list were generated in FirmGen (see firmware/docs/design/firmware-design-spec.md and .craftif/ in the repo). FirmGen credits ran out at task 7 of 11, so the remaining build fixes, indicators, button, and threshold tuning were completed manually with an AI assistant. Thresholds were tuned on hardware (the original PRD used 2x for the anomaly).

Verified on hardware: baseline learning, green/orange/red states, recovery, and button recalibration. Not verified: the 70 °C temperature alarm, the sensor-fault path, UART serial output (no USB-serial adapter), and a full FirmGen traceability report (not exportable after credits ran out).
